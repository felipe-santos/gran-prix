Point your phone at a leaf and learn its name

Pocketherb recognises more than 3,000 garden and wayside plants from a single photo, then tells you how to look after them or whether to pull them out.

Get it for phones Get it for tablets

How it works

  1. Take a photo. Frame one leaf or flower in daylight for the best match.
  2. Read the result. You get the common name, the botanical name and a confidence score.
  3. Save to your garden. Keep a list of what grows where, with care reminders for each.

A library that grows with you

Every plant has a short profile covering light, water, soil and common problems. Profiles are written by volunteer gardeners and checked by a botanist before they appear in the app.

Questions people ask

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. The most common 500 plants are stored on your device.

Is it free?

Identification is free. A small yearly plan adds unlimited garden lists.
