My starter is two weeks old and has started to smell sharply like nail polish remover. It still bubbles after feeding, but the top has a thin grey liquid. I feed it equal parts flour and water once a day and keep it on the kitchen counter.

Is it ruined, or can I rescue it? I would rather not throw it out and begin again.

That smell means the starter is hungry. The grey liquid is just hooch, and you can stir it in or pour it off. Move to two feedings a day for a few days and discard more before each feed.
