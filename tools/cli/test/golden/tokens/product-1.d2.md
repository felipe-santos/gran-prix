## About this kettle

The Larkspur is a lightweight stainless kettle designed for camp stoves and small fires. A wide base spreads heat evenly, and the folding handle locks in two positions so that it stays cool enough to grip while pouring.

Inside, a measuring scale is etched in both litres and cups. The lid seats firmly, and a tiny steam vent keeps the spout from spitting when the water reaches a rolling boil.
